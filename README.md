Online Shopping Cart | Python | DSA

Features:
. User Registration & Login

. Browse products by categories (Clothes, Makeup, Haircare, Skincare)

. Add items to cart with quantity tracking

. View cart with total price calculation

. Checkout functionality

Tech Stack:
. Python 3.x

. Tkinter (GUI)

. Pillow (Image processing)

How to Use:
. Register a new account

. Login with credentials

. Browse categories → Click "View More"

. Click "Add to Cart" on products

. "View Cart" → "Checkout" to complete purchase

Requirements:
. Python 3.6+

. 256MB RAM

. Images folder with product images (PNG format)
